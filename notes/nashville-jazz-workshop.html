nashville jazz workshop

This place is honestly one of my favorite nonprofits to volunteer at, and I don't know why I don't come here more often. A real case of cognitive dissonance on my part. I truly belong here, and belonging is something I often struggle to feel in other environments. The music really brings us all together - all staff members of Nashville Jazz Workshop are musicians themselves and work at the nonprofit on the side. I love jazz, they love jazz, everyone loves jazz.

I was tasked with guiding people to their tables as they came in to watch the show, and I got the chance to have some conversations with community members as I guided them to their table. There were so many incredibly cool elderly people that I found inspiring. Our conversations were around playing instruments, comparing the piano to the ukulele, talking about how amazing Nashville Jazz Workshop is, etc. I also checked people in when the main staff member was busy. When the show started I didn't have as much stuff to do but people would still come up to me asking where the nursing room is etc. so I got to help them (there is no nursing room but we found a place). After the show ended, we cleaned the place. People were so happy that they were just talking with each other and not leaving the building, so we eventually had to kick them out.

The performer was apparently raised going to church 3-4 nights a week and all of Sunday, and his music is deeply inspired by church music. He explained to us what a testimony service is, and then he played songs that felt like testimony service almost? I have been to church only 2 times in my life, so I don't know exactly, but the room felt like church when that was happening. I could feel god in my heart for a few minutes. I felt so overjoyed at that moment that I almost cried. I think my pursuit of finding god has been an ongoing theme for the past few years and I still can't feel god outside of rare occasions like this. During that timeframe, I also felt very connected to the room, and everyone else seemed to be transported to some kind of abstract concept of church in the same way. Certainly a very unusual Friday night. I couldn't have asked for anything better. This was so cool.
