Hi! I'm Sefika.

I work on AI safety: evaluations, reinforcement learning, and the governance questions autonomous agents raise.

I study Computer Science & Math at Vanderbilt as an Ingram Scholar, where I founded and lead the Vanderbilt AI Safety Initiative. With a 6-person team, we're running a fellowship for 25 fellows.

Right now I'm researching agentic jailbreaking of educational LLM apps with Prof. Meiyi Ma at Vanderbilt's Institute for Software Integrated Systems. This summer I did AI research at Good Start Labs, training models with RL and building evals. I found that headline benchmark gains can hide reward shortcutting. I also co-wrote the AUTONOMY Act, a policy memo on who is liable when AI agents make political deepfakes, and co-founded Invariant, infrastructure for autonomous agents.

Before that I built underwater robots that dive 200m, a multi-agent RL environment for Polytopia that won HackMIT, a fermionic quantum information library, and, at 17, a disinformation game funded by the US State Department.
